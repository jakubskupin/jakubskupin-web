Most of your catalogue never makes it into the AI answer.

Not because those are worse products. Because the data describing them is thin, and answer engines read data, not marketing copy.

Ranketta measures where your products show up across every engine, finds what keeps them out, rewrites the weak fields and pushes them back to the feed.

The whole loop in 73 seconds. No voiceover, the wave is visibility.
